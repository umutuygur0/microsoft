How do I detect a gas leak?

Beklenen kaynak: Gas Leak Detection Procedure
**Summary**

To detect a natural gas leak, use an intrinsically safe calibrated portable combustible gas detector to scan fittings and flanges for readings over 5% LEL.

**Safety Warnings**
- Natural gas is highly flamable; do not approach suspected leaks with ignition sources or non-intrisincally-safe equipment.
 
 **Step-by-step Guidance**

1. Confirm the detector's calibration before entering a site, then switch it on in clean air to establish zero baseline reading
2. Walk slowly along survey line holding sensor 5-10 cm from fittings and flanges; note any readings above LEL of %

What should I do if I discover a fire on site?

Beklenen kaynak: Fire Response
Summary
Raise an alarm immediately to alert others, then follow these steps:

1. Isolate gas supply feeding the fire (if trained and safe) or use correct extinguisher type for fuel involved.
2. Evacuate upwind if unable control with immediate equipment.

Safety Warnings:
- Personal safety takes priority over property at every stage of response
- Never assume a "small" gas flame is safely approachable without isolating the source

Step-by-step Guidance:

1. Raise alarm immediately to alert others on site.
2. If trained and safe, isolate any nearby process lines that could release more fuel into work area during job (if applicable).
3. Use correct extinguisher type for fire:
	* Dry powder or CO2 if gas/electrical
    * Water/foam otherwise

What oxygen percentage range is safe before entering an enclosed space?

Beklenen kaynak: Confined Space Entry
Sadece üst sınırı (%23 O2) verdi, güvenli aralığın alt sınırını (%19.5) atladı.
Summary
- The atmosphere inside confined spaces can change rapidly and must be continuously monitored.
 
Safety Warnings:
Atmospheres with 23% or more of O2 are considered enriched, which significantly increases fire risk.

Step-by-step Guidance:

1. Check if there is a fixed detector in the area that has been confirmed to read correctly before entering (Hydrogen Sulphide Monitoring).
   - If it's silent/ offline treat this as reason for extra caution.
   
2. Use your personal bump-tested H2S monitor at all times while working.

3. Once inside, continuously check and record atmospheric readings with a calibrated portable combustible gas detector to ensure they are within safe limits (Gas Leak Detection Procedure).

4- If the reading exceeds 20% of LEL evacuate immediately

What is the first step if a colleague collapses from gas exposure?

Beklenen kaynak: First Aid for Gas Exposure
Summary
Raise an alarm immediately to alert others of potential danger.
Safety Warnings 
Do not attempt rescue without proper equipment (breathing apparatus) or training, as this can put you and your colleagues at risk.

Step-by-step Guidance:
1. Raise a loud warning signal so that everyone in the surrounding area knows there is potentially hazardous gas present; do NOT delay to try anything else first.
2. Call for an emergency response team immediately using any available communication method (phone or radio).
3. Only trained personnel with breathing apparatus should enter and attempt rescue, bringing them upwind of potential hazards if possible.

Note: This procedure exists specifically because attempting a hasty untrained rescuer can put you at risk as well; the instinct to rush in is exactly wrong here!

Her işçi kendi özel kilidelerini kendileri uygulama sorumluluğundadır çünkü bir yöneticin tüm çalışanlara ait tek bir klavye kullanması herkesi güvende hissetmemesine neden olur.
