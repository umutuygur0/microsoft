How do I detect a gas leak?

Beklenen kaynak: Gas Leak Detection Procedure
**Summary**

To detect a natural gas leak, use a calibrated portable combustible gas detector to scan fittings and flanges for readings over 5% LEL.

**Safety Warnings**
 
- Do NOT approach suspected leaks from downwind.
  
Natural Gas is highly flamable; avoid ignition sources within the area. If reading exceeds 20%, evacuate immediately with an exclusion zone of at least a metre radius before further action can be taken.


To detect gas leak, follow these steps:

1. Ensure detector calibration and bump-test it beforehand.

2. Switch on in clean air to establish zero baseline.
 
3. Walk slowly near fittings (5-10 cm), noting readings above 5% LEL as potential leaks for closer inspection.



**Step-by-step Guidance**

To detect a gas leak, follow these steps:

1. Ensure the detector is calibrated and bump-tested before use.

2. Switch on in clean air to establish zero baseline.
 
3. Walk slowly near fittings (flanges & joints), holding sensor 5-10 cm away from them.



Note: If reading exceeds LEL of more than a fifth, evacuate area immediately with an exclusion zone set up first
