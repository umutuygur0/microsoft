# Cathodic Protection Survey

## Purpose
Verifies that the cathodic protection (CP) system is preventing corrosion on
buried and submerged pipelines.

## Safety Warnings
- CP test stations may carry induced AC voltage; use insulated tools and gloves.
- Do not disconnect bonding cables on pipelines with induced current without authorisation.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Pipe-to-soil potential more negative than -0.85 V (with respect to a
  copper/copper-sulphate reference electrode).
- Avoid potentials more negative than -1.2 V to prevent coating disbondment.

## Procedure
1. Connect the reference electrode to the soil above the pipeline.
2. Measure the pipe-to-soil potential at each test post.
3. Record the on and instant-off potentials.
4. Compare readings against the protection criterion.
5. Investigate any post reading less negative than -0.85 V.
6. Check rectifier output current and voltage at the transformer-rectifier unit.
7. Log all readings and trend against previous surveys.

## Reference
Maintenance Standard MS-21, Section 2 — Cathodic Protection.
