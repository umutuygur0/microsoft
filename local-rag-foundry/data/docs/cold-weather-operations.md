# Cold Weather Operations

## Purpose
Precautions for operating gas field equipment in freezing conditions to prevent
hydrate formation, freezing, and personnel harm.

## Safety Warnings
- Ice can hide leaks and block relief paths; inspect vents and reliefs for ice.
- Frozen ground and icy walkways increase slip and vehicle hazards.
- Metal surfaces can cause cold burns; wear insulated gloves.

## Hydrate and Freeze Prevention
- Maintain methanol or glycol injection at the required rate.
- Keep instrument and impulse lines heat-traced and insulated.
- Drain low points where water can accumulate and freeze.

## Procedure
1. Confirm heat tracing and insulation are energised and intact.
2. Check methanol or glycol injection rates and tank levels.
3. Inspect vents, drains, and relief valves for ice blockage.
4. Monitor for pressure drops that may indicate a hydrate plug forming.
5. Never apply direct flame to thaw frozen equipment; use approved heating.
6. Increase inspection frequency during extreme cold.
