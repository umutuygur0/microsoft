# Gas Compressor Maintenance

## Purpose
Routine maintenance of reciprocating and centrifugal gas compressors to maintain
reliability and prevent failures.

## Safety Warnings
- Isolate and depressurise the compressor and apply LOTO before any work.
- Hot surfaces and rotating parts remain hazardous after shutdown; allow cooling.
- Vent trapped gas from cylinders and seals to a safe location.

## Routine Checks
- Lubricating oil level, pressure, and temperature.
- Vibration readings against the alarm and trip set points.
- Seal gas and vent system operation.
- Cooling water or air flow and temperature.
- Suction and discharge pressures and temperatures.

## Procedure
1. Record baseline running parameters before shutdown.
2. Isolate, depressurise, and lock out the machine.
3. Inspect valves, rings, and seals per the maintenance schedule.
4. Check and top up lubricating oil; replace filters as due.
5. Verify instrument and trip functions on test.
6. Complete a controlled restart and confirm parameters are within limits.

## Reference
Maintenance Standard MS-25, Section 3 — Rotating Equipment.
