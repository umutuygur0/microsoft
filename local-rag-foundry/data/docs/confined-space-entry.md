# Confined Space Entry

## Purpose
Defines the controls for entering confined spaces such as separator vessels,
storage tanks, and pits where atmosphere and access are restricted.

## Safety Warnings
- Never enter a confined space without a valid entry permit and gas test.
- A trained standby attendant must remain outside for the entire entry.
- Atmospheres can change rapidly; continuous monitoring is mandatory.

## Atmospheric Test Limits
- Oxygen: 19.5% to 23.5%.
- Combustible gas: below 10% of the Lower Explosive Limit (LEL).
- Hydrogen sulphide (H2S): below 5 ppm.
- Carbon monoxide: below 25 ppm.

## Procedure
1. Isolate, drain, purge, and where required ventilate the space.
2. Complete atmospheric testing at top, middle, and bottom levels.
3. Issue the confined space entry permit with named entrants and attendant.
4. Set up rescue equipment and communications before entry.
5. Maintain continuous gas monitoring throughout the work.
6. Log entry and exit times for every person.
7. Close the permit when work is complete and all personnel are out.
