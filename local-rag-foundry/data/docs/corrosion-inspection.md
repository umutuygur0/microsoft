# Corrosion Inspection

## Purpose
Routine inspection to detect and assess corrosion on pipwork, vessels, and
structures before it threatens integrity.

## Safety Warnings
- Corroded components may be weaker than they appear; do not apply load or pressure
  to suspect items.
- Wear appropriate PPE when removing insulation, which may hide corrosion under
  insulation (CUI).

## Inspection Methods
- Visual inspection for pitting, blistering, and surface rust.
- Ultrasonic thickness (UT) measurement to check remaining wall thickness.
- Removal of insulation at sample points to check for CUI.

## Procedure
1. Clean the surface to bare metal at each measurement point.
2. Record wall thickness readings and compare to the minimum allowable value.
3. Photograph and map any pitting or general wall loss.
4. Flag any reading below the minimum for immediate engineering assessment.
5. Reinstate coatings and insulation after inspection.
6. Update the corrosion monitoring records and trend the data.

## Reference
Maintenance Standard MS-20, Section 4 — Corrosion Management.
