# Emergency Shutdown (ESD) Procedure

## Purpose
The Emergency Shutdown system brings the facility to a safe state during an
abnormal or hazardous event such as a major gas release, fire, or loss of control.

## Safety Warnings
- Personnel safety takes priority over equipment protection at all times.
- Never reset an ESD trip until the root cause has been identified and cleared.
- Assume all lines may still contain pressure until proven otherwise.

## When to Activate
- Confirmed uncontrolled gas release.
- Fire or explosion on site.
- Detection of gas above 60% LEL in an occupied area.
- Loss of critical control or instrument air.

## Procedure
1. Activate the nearest ESD push button or trigger the logic from the control room.
2. Confirm the shutdown valves (SDVs) have closed and the field is isolated.
3. Confirm the blowdown sequence has depressurised the affected sections to flare.
4. Account for all personnel at the designated muster point.
5. Notify the shift supervisor and emergency services if required.
6. Do not re-enter the area until the atmosphere has been confirmed safe by gas testing.

## Reset
- Reset only on written authorisation from the responsible authority.
- Perform a controlled, staged restart following the start-up procedure.
