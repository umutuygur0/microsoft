# Fire Response

## Purpose
Immediate actions for personnel on discovering a fire on a gas installation.

## Safety Warnings
- Never fight a gas fire by extinguishing the flame unless the gas can be isolated;
  an unburned gas cloud can re-ignite explosively.
- Raise the alarm before attempting any first-response action.
- Personal safety and evacuation take priority over property.

## Procedure
1. Raise the alarm and activate the nearest fire/ESD call point.
2. If trained and safe, isolate the gas supply feeding the fire.
3. Attack only small, incipient fires with the correct extinguisher.
4. Use dry powder or CO2 on gas and electrical fires — never water on live electrics.
5. Evacuate upwind to the muster point if the fire cannot be controlled.
6. Account for all personnel and report to the incident controller.
7. Do not re-enter until the area is declared safe.

## Extinguisher Selection
- Gas fire: dry powder.
- Electrical fire: CO2.
- Solid combustibles: water or foam.
