# First Aid for Gas Exposure

## Purpose
Immediate first-aid actions for a casualty overcome by toxic or asphyxiant gas.

## Safety Warnings
- Do NOT enter the contaminated area to rescue without breathing apparatus and a
  trained team; rescuers becoming casualties is a common cause of multiple deaths.
- Assume the atmosphere is still hazardous until confirmed clear.

## Procedure
1. Raise the alarm and call for the emergency response team.
2. Only a team with breathing apparatus removes the casualty to fresh air, upwind.
3. Check for breathing; if absent, begin CPR if trained.
4. If breathing, place in the recovery position and keep the airway clear.
5. Administer oxygen if trained and equipment is available.
6. Keep the casualty warm and monitor until medical help arrives.
7. Record the gas involved and exposure time for medical staff.

## H2S Specific
- H2S exposure can cause delayed lung injury; all exposed persons must be
  medically assessed even if they appear to recover.

Field Safety Manual, Section 6.2 — Emergency First Aid.
