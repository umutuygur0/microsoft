# Flare System Operation

## Purpose
Describes safe operation and monitoring of the flare system that safely burns
relieved and vented gas.

## Safety Warnings
- Never isolate the flare header while any equipment can relieve to it.
- A flame-out with continued gas flow releases unburned gas — treat as an emergency.
- Maintain the purge gas flow at all times to prevent air ingress and flashback.

## Monitoring
- Confirm the pilot flame is lit using the pilot thermocouple and flare camera.
- Maintain minimum purge gas flow per the operating envelope.
- Monitor knock-out drum level to prevent liquid carry-over to the flare tip.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the pilot flame status before and during flaring events.
2. Verify the knock-out drum level is within limits and the pump is available.
3. Monitor flare gas flow and smoke; adjust steam or air assist to reduce smoking.
4. On pilot flame-out, re-ignite immediately using the ignition system.
5. If re-ignition fails with gas flowing, initiate the emergency procedure.
6. Log flaring volumes for environmental reporting.
