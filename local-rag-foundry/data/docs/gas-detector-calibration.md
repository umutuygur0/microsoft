# Gas Detector Calibration

## Purpose
Ensures portable and fixed gas detectors read accurately by regular bump testing
and calibration.

## Safety Warnings
- Use only certified calibration gas within its expiry date.
- Calibrate in a well-ventilated area away from ignition sources.
- A detector that fails calibration must be removed from service immediately.

## Definitions
- Bump test: a brief exposure to test gas to confirm the sensor and alarms respond.
- Calibration: adjusting the detector reading to match a known gas concentration.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the calibration gas type and concentration match the sensor.
2. Perform a fresh-air zero in clean air.
3. Apply the calibration gas at the specified flow rate.
4. Allow the reading to stabilise and adjust the span to the certified value.
5. Confirm the low and high alarms activate at the correct set points.
6. Record the calibration date, gas lot number, and result.
7. Apply a calibration due sticker and update the equipment register.

## Frequency
- Bump test: before each day of use.
- Full calibration: at least every 6 months, or per manufacturer guidance.

## Reference
Maintenance Standard MS-18, Section 1 — Gas Detection Equipment.
