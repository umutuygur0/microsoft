# Gas Leak Detection Procedure

## Purpose
This procedure describes how to detect and confirm a natural gas leak on a
pipeline or wellhead installation using a calibrated portable gas detector.

## Safety Warnings
- Natural gas is highly flammable. Do NOT use any ignition source, mobile phone,
  or non-intrinsically-safe equipment within a suspected leak zone.
- Always approach a suspected leak from upwind.
- If the reading exceeds 20% of the Lower Explosive Limit (LEL), evacuate the
  area immediately and establish a 50 metre exclusion zone.

## Equipment
- Calibrated portable combustible gas detector (checked within the last 24 hours)
- Intrinsically safe torch
- Leak detection fluid (soap solution) for pinpointing

## Procedure
1. Confirm the detector is calibrated and bump-tested before entering the site.
2. Switch on the detector in clean air to establish a zero baseline.
3. Walk the survey line slowly, holding the sensor 5 to 10 cm from fittings,
   flanges, and joints.
4. Note any reading above 5% LEL as a potential leak location.
5. Apply leak detection fluid to the suspected joint; bubbling confirms escaping gas.
6. Record the location, LEL reading, and time on the inspection form.
7. Isolate the affected section and raise a work order for repair.
