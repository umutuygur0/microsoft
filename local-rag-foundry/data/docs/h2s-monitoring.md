# Hydrogen Sulphide (H2S) Monitoring

## Purpose
Describes monitoring and response for hydrogen sulphide, a toxic gas found in
sour gas fields.

## Safety Warnings
- H2S is toxic and can be fatal within minutes at high concentrations.
- Do not rely on smell; H2S deadens the sense of smell at dangerous levels.
- If a high alarm sounds, hold your breath, leave the area upwind, and raise the alarm.

## Alarm Set Points
- Low alarm: 5 ppm.
- High alarm: 10 ppm.
- Immediately Dangerous to Life or Health (IDLH): 100 ppm.

## Procedure
1. Wear a bump-tested personal H2S monitor in all sour-gas areas.
2. Confirm fixed detector readings before entering a work area.
3. On a low alarm, stop work and assess the source.
4. On a high alarm, evacuate upwind to the muster point immediately.
5. Do not attempt rescue without breathing apparatus and a trained team.
6. Report all alarms, even brief ones, to the shift supervisor.

## Reference
Field Safety Manual, Section 2.4 — Toxic Gas Hazards.
