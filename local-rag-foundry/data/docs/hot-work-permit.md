# Hot Work Permit

## Purpose
Controls any work producing heat, sparks, or flame — welding, grinding, cutting —
in areas where flammable gas may be present.

## Safety Warnings
- No hot work may begin until a gas test confirms the area is below 1% LEL.
- A dedicated fire watch must be present during work and for 30 minutes after.
- Remove or cover all combustible material within 15 metres.

## Procedure
1. Complete a gas test immediately before issuing the permit.
2. Verify fire extinguishers and a charged hose are in position.
3. Isolate any nearby process lines that could release gas.
4. Brief the fire watch on alarm and evacuation actions.
5. Perform periodic gas re-tests at the interval stated on the permit.
6. Stop work immediately if gas is detected above 1% LEL.
7. Maintain the fire watch for 30 minutes after work stops, then close the permit.

## Reference
Field Safety Manual, Section 5.4 — Hot Work.
