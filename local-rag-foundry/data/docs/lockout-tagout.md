# Lockout / Tagout (LOTO)

## Purpose
Ensures that energy sources are isolated and cannot be re-energised while
maintenance is performed on equipment.

## Safety Warnings
- Each person working on the equipment must apply their own personal lock.
- Never remove another worker's lock or tag.
- Verify zero energy state before starting work — assume energised until proven.

## Energy Sources to Isolate
- Process pressure (gas and liquid).
- Electrical supply to motors and actuators.
- Stored mechanical energy (springs, counterweights).
- Instrument air and hydraulic pressure.

## Procedure
1. Identify all energy sources feeding the equipment.
2. Shut down the equipment using the normal stopping procedure.
3. Isolate each energy source and apply locks and tags.
4. Release or block any stored energy (bleed pressure, discharge capacitors).
5. Verify isolation by attempting a controlled start (try-out) — it must not run.
6. Perform the maintenance work.
7. Remove locks in reverse order only when the area is clear.
