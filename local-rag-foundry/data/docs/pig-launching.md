# Pipeline Pig Launching

## Purpose
Safe procedure for launching a cleaning or inspection pig into a pipeline using
a pig launcher trap.

## Safety Warnings
- The launcher is a pressure vessel; never open the closure until it is confirmed
  fully depressurised and the pressure gauge reads zero.
- Stand clear of the closure door when opening — trapped pressure can fling it open.
- Confirm the vent and drain lines are routed to a safe location.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the launcher is isolated from the main line and depressurised.
2. Open the closure and inspect the barrel for cleanliness and damage.
3. Load the pig and confirm correct orientation.
4. Close and lock the closure; confirm the interlock is engaged.
5. Equalise pressure across the launcher and open the kicker line.
6. Open the main valve and use kicker flow to launch the pig.
7. Confirm pig launch via the signaller, then restore normal valve line-up.
