# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements

## Purpose
Defines the minimum PPE required for personnel entering the gas field operational area.

## Mandatory PPE (all areas)
- Flame-retardant (FR) coveralls rated to the site standard.
- Safety helmet with chin strap.
- Safety glasses with side shields.
- Steel-toe, anti-static safety boots.
- Hearing protection in designated high-noise zones (above 85 dB).

## Task-Specific PPE
- Gas surveys: personal H2S monitor and combustible gas detector.
- Chemical handling: chemical-resistant gloves and face shield.
- Working at height: full-body harness with a certified lanyard.
- Hot work: welding shield, FR gauntlets, and a fire watch.

## H2S Areas
- A personal H2S monitor is mandatory in all sour-gas areas.
- Escape breathing apparatus must be carried where H2S may exceed 10 ppm.
- Alarm set points: low at 5 ppm, high at 10 ppm.

## Inspection
- Inspect all PPE before each use; remove damaged items from service.
- Gas detectors must show a valid calibration date within the last 6 months.
