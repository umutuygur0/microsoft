# Pipeline Pressure Testing

## Purpose
Hydrostatic and pneumatic pressure testing verifies the integrity of a pipeline
section before it is returned to service.

## Safety Warnings
- Pneumatic testing stores large amounts of energy. Keep all personnel outside
  the calculated exclusion radius during pressurisation.
- Never exceed the maximum allowable test pressure (1.5x the design pressure
  for hydrostatic tests) stated on the pipeline data sheet.
- Depressurise slowly; rapid release can damage seals and injure personnel.

## Test Parameters
- Hydrostatic test medium: clean water with corrosion inhibitor.
- Standard hold time: 4 hours minimum after temperature stabilisation.
- Acceptable pressure drop: less than 0.5% of test pressure over the hold period.

## Procedure
1. Isolate and blank the test section; verify all valves are in the correct position.
2. Fill the section, venting air from the highest point.
3. Raise pressure in 25% increments, holding 10 minutes at each step to inspect
   for leaks.
4. Reach test pressure and begin the timed hold.
5. Record pressure and temperature every 15 minutes.
6. On completion, depressurise gradually and drain the test medium to an approved point.
7. Complete the test certificate and attach the pressure chart.

## Reference
Maintenance Standard MS-12, Section 3 — Integrity Testing.
