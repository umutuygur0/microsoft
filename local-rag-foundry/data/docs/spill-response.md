# Liquid Spill Response

## Purpose
Immediate response to a spill of hydrocarbon condensate, chemicals, or produced
water to protect people and the environment.

## Safety Warnings
- Hydrocarbon spills create a flammable vapour hazard; eliminate ignition sources.
- Wear chemical-resistant PPE appropriate to the spilled substance.
- Do not enter a vapour cloud without gas monitoring.

## Procedure
1. Raise the alarm and, if safe, stop the source of the spill.
2. Eliminate ignition sources in and around the spill area.
3. Contain the spill using booms, bunds, or absorbent materials.
4. Prevent the spill from reaching drains, watercourses, or soil.
5. Recover the liquid using pumps or absorbents into approved containers.
6. Notify the environmental coordinator and record the spill volume and cause.
7. Dispose of contaminated material through an approved waste route.

## Spill Kit Contents
- Absorbent pads and booms, non-sparking shovel, drain covers, and waste bags.
