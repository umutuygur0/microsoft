# Valve Inspection and Maintenance

## Purpose
Routine inspection and lubrication of isolation and control valves to ensure
reliable operation and tight shut-off.

## Safety Warnings
- Confirm the valve is isolated and depressurised before any hands-on work.
- Follow the lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedure and verify zero energy state.
- Some valve greases are under pressure in the injection system; wear eye
  protection when using a grease gun.

## Inspection Checklist
1. Check the body and bonnet for external corrosion or leakage.
2. Operate the valve through a full open-close cycle; note any stiffness.
3. Verify the position indicator matches the actual valve state.
4. Inspect the stem seal and gland packing for signs of weeping.
5. Check actuator air supply pressure (for pneumatic actuators): 5.5 to 7 bar.

## Lubrication
- Inject the specified valve grease through the sealant fitting until back
  pressure is felt.
- Do not over-inject; excessive sealant can jam the ball or gate.
- Record grease type, quantity, and date on the maintenance log.

Maintenance Standard MS-15, Section 2 — Valve Servicing.
