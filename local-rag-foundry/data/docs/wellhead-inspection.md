# Wellhead Inspection

## Purpose
Routine inspection of the wellhead and Christmas tree to confirm integrity and
correct valve status.

## Safety Warnings
- The wellhead contains high-pressure hydrocarbons; never loosen fittings under pressure.
- Confirm the master valve and wing valve positions before any intervention.
- Wear H2S monitoring in sour wells.

## Inspection Checklist
1. Check for hydrocarbon leaks at flanges, valve stems, and fittings.
2. Record tubing head and casing head pressures.
3. Verify all valves are in their correct operating position.
4. Inspect the pressure gauges for damage and valid calibration.
5. Check the grease and hydraulic levels on the actuated valves.
6. Confirm the surface safety valve (SSV) closes on test.
7. Inspect the wellhead cellar for accumulated gas or liquids.
